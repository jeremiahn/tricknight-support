Support Privacy

Privacy Policy.

Trick Night does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

Effective November 21, 2026

No accounts.

There's nothing to sign up for or sign in to.

No internet.

Trick Night works fully offline and doesn't send data anywhere.

No tracking.

No ads, analytics, crash reporting or third-party code that collects data.

What stays on your iPhone.

Trick Night keeps your game in progress and your settings (the game and difficulty you last chose) on your iPhone, so you can pick up where you left off. This information never leaves your device and the developer can't see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, that backup may include Trick Night's saved game, the same as for other apps. Apple handles those backups under your Apple Account settings.

Children.

Trick Night doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

App Store.

Apple, not the developer, handles your download of Trick Night under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Changes.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
