Common questions.

Which games are included?

Spades, Hearts, Euchre, four-player partnership Pinochle and Two-Handed Pinochle. Choose one on the start screen, pick a difficulty and tap Deal.

Who is my partner?

In Spades, Euchre and Pinochle your partner is the computer player sitting across the table. Hearts is every player for themselves, and Two-Handed Pinochle is you against one opponent.

How does difficulty work?

Choose Easy, Normal or Hard. Each level has four computer players, seated at random each game. Easy players make beginner mistakes; Hard players think through how the rest of the hand could go before every card.

How do I play a card?

Tap a card to raise it, then tap it again to play it. Cards you can't play right now are dimmed, and tapping one tells you why, such as "You must follow suit."

How do I learn the rules?

During a game, tap the book icon at the top of the screen. How to Play covers every game: bidding, passing, trump, meld and scoring.

What rules does Spades use?

Partnership Spades to 500, with Nil (±100) and Blind Nil (±200, only when your team is 100 or more behind). Every 10 bags costs 100, and a team that falls to −200 loses.

What rules does Euchre use?

Partnership Euchre to 10 with the 24-card deck, right and left bowers, going alone, and stick the dealer: if everyone passes twice, the dealer must name trump.

What rules does Pinochle use?

Single-deck partnership Pinochle to 1500: bid from 250, the high bidder names trump, partners pass four cards each way, then meld and play. Aces, tens and kings count 10 each. Two-Handed Pinochle plays to 1000 with a stock to draw from.

Is there help while I play?

Yes. Spades suggests a bid, Suggest picks cards to pass or bury, and Pinochle shows your meld for each trump suit while you bid.

Does it work with VoiceOver?

Yes. Every card, bid, pass and trick is announced in plain words, and double-tapping a card plays it. Larger text and Reduce Motion are supported too.

Is my game saved?

Yes. Trick Night saves after every card, so you can close the app and pick up mid-hand. Deleting the app also deletes the saved game.

Do I need internet?

No. Trick Night works fully offline, and the computer players run on your iPhone.

Ads, purchases or betting?

None. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no wagering; scores are game points only.

Which devices?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
